# Accessible and inclusive gameplay

Read `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `docs/USER_JOURNEYS.md` when behavior needs specifics. Provide meaningful text for timer/reveal, live announcements that avoid alert spam, focus return after dialogs, accessible table/chart alternative, 44px mobile tap targets, keyboard alternatives to drag/draw, non-color-only results, sound/motion controls and high contrast. Every optional personal question has Pass/Skip; webcam not required. Untimed or adaptive modes must be fair. Test keyboard-only and screen readers before release. Do not treat automated accessibility scanner alone as full verification.
