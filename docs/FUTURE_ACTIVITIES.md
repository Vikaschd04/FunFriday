# Expansion Library - Games and Activities Beyond the First Six

Build only once the core room engine and six MVP games work reliably. Classify by objective and interaction pattern. Participant ranges are product-design targets, not verified load capacities.

| Activity | Bonding goal | Mechanics | Format / size | Complexity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Common Ground Quest | Find unexpected similarities | Breakout groups find 3 shared interests | 4-60 breakout | M |
| Human Bingo | Meet multiple colleagues | Find people who match voluntary safe prompts | 6-100 | M |
| Crowd Consensus | Understand team perspectives | Guess most-selected poll response | 4-200 | S |
| Emoji Decoder | Fast creative laughter | Solve emoji movie/phrase sequences | 3-100 | S |
| Word Sprint | Collaborative agility | Category/word race with team scoring | 4-100 | M |
| Story Chain | Group improvisation | Add sentences to shared story | 3-30 breakout | S |
| Drawing Relay | Playful co-creation | Pass evolving drawing between groups | 4-30 breakout | L |
| Mystery Mission | Deep teamwork | Distributed clues and timed puzzle locks | 4-60 breakout | XL |
| Team Detective | Communication | Each participant receives part of a clue | 4-60 breakout | XL |
| Desert Island Decisions | Discussion and priorities | Debate to agree on 5 items | 3-60 breakout | S |
| Virtual Scavenger Hunt | Physical-world play | Solve clue, find optional safe objects | 4-100 | M |
| Build the Impossible | Imagination and pitching | Random feature cards + team pitch | 4-60 breakout | M |
| Virtual Tambola | Casual community competition | Digital ticket, number stream, prize claims | 5-200 | M |
| Guess the Sound | Shared surprise | Play licensed snippets or self-made sounds | 3-100 | M |
| Fact or Fiction | Curiosity and conversation | Vote which fictional statement is real | 3-200 | S |
| Team Superlatives | Appreciation | Opt-in positive nominations, no exclusion | 5-60 | M |
| Photo Story Prompt | Creative sharing | Optional photo or anonymous illustrations | 3-30 | M |
| Reverse Charades | Non-verbal collaboration | Several teammates give prompts simultaneously | 4-30 | M |
| Digital Puzzle Race | Collaboration | Solve word/picture puzzles under time | 4-100 | M |
| Match the Desk Object | Low-pressure introduction | Guess anonymous shared everyday item | 4-30 | M |
| Coffee Roulette | 1:1 connection | Voluntary matching + conversation prompts | 2-200 | L (scheduling) |
| Appreciation Wall | Positive recognition | Opt-in gratitude notes to team | 2-200 | S |
| Show and Tell | Discovery of hobbies | Share an object, story, or text-only note | 2-30 | S |
| Collaborative Playlist | Shared interests | Nominate songs via licensed music links | 3-100 | M |
| Culture Swap | Inclusion | Share foods/traditions/arts voluntarily | 4-60 | S |
| New Joiner Welcome | Onboarding ease | Conversation starters and paired prompts | 3-30 | S |
| Memory Wall | Shared team identity | Optional drawings/reactions after games | 3-200 | M |
| Friday Pulse | Improve future events | Anonymous aggregate enjoyment poll | 2-200 | S |

S=small, M=medium, L=large, XL=high-complexity. Social activities require even more care with consent and inclusion than straightforward trivia. Avoid mandatory identity, background or personal-life disclosure.

## Selection algorithm (future optional)

Choose activities via participant count, allotted minutes, recent sessions, host's desired energy, accessibility needs, language and willingness for personal sharing. Start with rule-based recommendations; AI is not necessary for MVP.
