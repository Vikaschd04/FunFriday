# Product Vision and Positioning

## Problem

Remote and hybrid colleagues often communicate only about tasks and deadlines. Informal relationships do not necessarily emerge from videoconferences. Team-building is fragmented between calendar invites, messaging tools, quiz sites, and manual facilitation, putting extra effort on organizers.

## Proposition

**FunFriday** makes casual, inclusive team connection easy: plan a session, invite your people, play real-time games, discover common ground, and leave with shared memories. Provide a frictionless host journey and delightful, low-pressure participant experience.

## Primary jobs to be done

1. When I manage a distributed group, I want to run a 15-60 minute event with minimal preparation and no software installs.
2. When I join a new team, I want easy ways to interact with coworkers without being forced to talk on camera or reveal personal details.
3. When I organize a recurring Fun Friday, I want new activities, reliable invites, fair team splits, and a session I can confidently host.
4. When I facilitate a larger group, I want everyone participating simultaneously rather than watching sequential turns.

## Audience

- Primary: Corporate team leads and HR/engagement organizers; participants in remote/hybrid teams of 15-20.
- Secondary: Onboarding teams, cross-functional groups, internal communities.
- Excluded initially: Public consumer social network, professional video conferencing service, employee performance scoring, LMS.

## Product values

- Human connection, inclusive fun, good faith competition, autonomy, privacy, originality, simplicity, memorable design.
- Success is *willing repeat participation and greater social ease*, not merely clicks or forced attendance.

## Brand identity

- Name (working): FunFriday; tagline: Connect Beyond Work. Play Together. Grow Together.
- Tone: warm, upbeat, witty but never juvenile or overly familiar.
- Character: a clever, stylish colleague who makes introductions comfortable.
- **Brand risk:** Similar names/products exist. Trademark/domain research and legal review required before public commercialization. Do not assert ownership of the name.

## Design north star

A premium collaboration SaaS meets thoughtful multiplayer party games. Make the app feel alive through tasteful motion, beautifully illustrated game cards, rich team colours and joyful micro-feedback while preserving crisp typography and enterprise trust.

## MVP north-star metric

Percentage of invited teams that voluntarily host a second event within 30 days. Supporting metrics: successful join rate, participant enjoyment, host prep time, completed sessions, and optional connection feedback.

## Boundaries

- Audio/video: use existing meetings by default; add native video only after testing necessity and cost.
- AI: optional later for curated game content. Human-reviewed packs are sufficient for MVP.
- Analytics: organization-level engagement; not surveillance of individual employee mood or personality.
