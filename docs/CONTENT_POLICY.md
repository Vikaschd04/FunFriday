# Content, Question Packs and Localization

## Content categories

Safe warm-up prompts, general trivia, visual word games, gentle workplace humor, common-interest discoveries, creative collaborations, fictional mysteries.

Avoid mandatory questions about protected traits, salaries, financial difficulties, religion, medical/mental health status, criminal histories, sexuality, immigration and political affiliation. Users may voluntarily share their own stories only with proper context and consent; do not solicit them.

## Editorial process

- Original game copy, artwork and licensed assets only; no copying third-party question banks without rights.
- Tag prompt with locale, language, sensitivity, difficulty, category, max length and moderation status.
- All custom corp packs reviewed by organizer/admin, with report and remove flows.
- Prefer globally accessible topics; provide culture-neutral alternatives.
- Review clue fairness (no inaccessible niche regional knowledge in otherwise general teams).

## Seed packs

- `warmup-global-en`: 30+ pairs/polls with safe follow-ups.
- `trivia-general-en`: 30+ questions with 4 options, one correct answer, explanation and source notes if needed.
- `caption-original-en`: 10 original or appropriately licensed illustrations.
- `sketch-friendly-en`: 100+ simple everyday concepts, not proper-name/IP-dependent prompts.

## AI content generation (post-MVP)

Optional AI-assisted editor generates draft packs, never silently publishes to employees. Require moderation, copyright filters and prompt-injection-aware validation. Disallow generating personal profiles based on actual employee data.
